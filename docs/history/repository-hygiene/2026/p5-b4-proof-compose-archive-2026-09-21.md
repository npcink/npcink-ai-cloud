package.json command, no CI job, and no runbook caller at archive time.

Accompanying reference removals in the same wave:

- `scripts/check-release-policy.sh` no longer `require_file`s

  compose into the release-policy fixture root.

  the two deleted contract test files (regenerated by
  `scripts/refresh-pytest-duration-weights.sh` regardless).
